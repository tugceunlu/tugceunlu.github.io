Research

How can generative AI support adults' learning without taking away their autonomy?

Adults learn at work, in community settings and on their own, often without a teacher. Generative AI is quickly becoming part of this learning, as a tutor, a content generator or a planning aid. My research asks what this means for adult learners and for the people who design adult education.

Questions I care about

  • In which adult learning settings is generative AI used, and in which roles?
  • How does it affect adults' autonomy, motivation and trust?
  • What do adults actually want from AI in their learning, and what do they reject?
  • How can AI tools be designed to support self-directed learning?

Current project

Generative AI in adult and lifelong learning: a systematic review

In progress · 2026–2027

A PRISMA-based systematic review of empirical studies on generative AI and large language models in adult, continuing, workplace and informal learning (2022–2026). The protocol will be registered on OSF and the review shared as a preprint.
